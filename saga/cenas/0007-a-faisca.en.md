# The Spark

[🇧🇷 Português](./0007-a-faisca.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

> Mission M0.7 · 2026-10-05

You did not forge the oracle. It arrives ready-made, through the widest crack in the Gauntlet: a voice taught far from here, by hands you will never see, with the words of a million strangers. You only have to speak its name, and it wakes.

You feed it ten sentences of your own. Crooked street sentences, full of sweet venom. Each one comes back judged in three colors: **light**, **shadow** or **grey**. Beside every verdict burns a number, the faith the oracle has in what it just said.

It reads the fury of the fallen system right. It gets the five-minute voice messages wrong: it calls a knife wrapped in honey light, and it is wrong with ninety parts in a hundred of certainty. Where it hesitates, the number drops below half, and there, you learn, is where you must look twice.

Then the Spark slips out of the Gauntlet: a copper ember that rises, turns and does not go out. It is the first power that obeys your voice.

The Throne laughs beneath you, a thousand throats at once.

— *Pretty. Borrowed, but pretty.* — The blades creak. — *Why learn to forge, heretic, when I can lend you a thousand ready-made oracles?*

That is the price. From now on, every time the Spark ignites, you will hear the Throne offer the shortcut.

Vael-Ithra watches the ember without blinking. When she speaks of the Throne, there is something in her voice that is not fear.

— It burned like that too, in the beginning.

You are still seated. But now the Gauntlet glows, and the Mist knows where you are.
