# ⛓️ The Saga of Aethelgard

[🇧🇷 Português](./README.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

This repository's [roadmap](../ROADMAP.en.md) is played as a **dark-fantasy narrative RPG**. Every study mission completed in the real world moves the story forward. Nothing from the future is revealed before its time.

> Grimdark and sci-fi fantasy: Elric of Melniboné, Dune, Dante's *Inferno*, Castlevania, The Lord of the Rings. Choices carry weight, allies may betray, power demands sacrifice.

The player plays in Portuguese; every chapter and scene is also published in English (`*.en.md`) so anyone can follow along.

## How to play

1. Pick the next roadmap mission (or ask: `/mestre status`).
2. Complete it in the real world, leaving **evidence** in the repo (note, code, notebook, `LOG.md`).
3. Report to the Master (*o Mestre*) in Claude Code:

| Command | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/mestre começar` | The Master awakens and hands out the first goal |
| `/mestre missão cumprida M0.1` | Checks the evidence → narrates the **scene** → updates XP, sheet and chronicle |
| `/mestre status` | Sheet, remaining chains, next goal |
| `/mestre side quest <what you did>` | Roadmap side quest (taken on your word) |
| `/mestre decisão <choice>` | Answers the end-of-chapter dilemma |

Every command, with examples and rules (challenge the boss, sanctuary, outposts, `/teach`…): **[COMANDOS.en.md](../COMANDOS.en.md)** · or `/mestre ajuda`.

## Rhythm

| Real-world event | In the story |
|---|---|
| Mission completed | Short **scene**: consequence, a detail of the world, sometimes an item |
| Weekly goal met | **Echo**: a whisper from the Throne, a rumor |
| Boss defeated | **Chapter**: the Overlord falls, a revelation, a **dilemma** |
| Weeks without studying | **The Mist advances** (never removes XP) |
| Achievement unlocked | **Legendary item** in the inventory |

## The map

**Prologue** (Phase 0) → **Circles 1–8** (Phases 1–8) → **Circle 9**: the signed job offer, when the skies open.
